# Commercial licensing

The skills in this repository are free for individuals and noncommercial use under
the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).

**If you are a company or using it for commercial advantage, you need a commercial
license.** That is deliberate: the work stays free for people learning and building
for themselves, and companies that derive commercial value from it pay for that value.

## Who needs a commercial license

You need one if you (or your organization) use this skill, its scripts, or its
guidance in a way primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary
compensation. Common cases:

- Using it in a company's product-development, design, or engineering workflow.
- Building it into a paid product, service, or internal tool.
- Consulting or agency work delivered to paying clients.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask.

## How to obtain one

Contact **Luke Oishi** — luke.oishi@gmail.com — with:

1. Your company name and a one-line description of the intended use.
2. Rough scale (team size / number of seats, or product context).

Licensing is currently handled case by case. Typical structures: a flat annual
per-company fee, or per-seat. Reasonable terms; the goal is fair value, not friction.
